Ziming Liu

Research Scientist, Bosch Research, Shanghai · ziming.liu@cn.bosch.com

I work as a research scientist at Bosch Research in Shanghai, working on AI algorithms for autonomous driving and robotic applications. I obtained my PhD in the ACENTAURI team at Inria, France, advised by Dr. Philippe Martinet and Dr. Ezio Malis. Before that I received a master's degree in software engineering from Beijing Institute of Technology (北京理工大学), supervised by Prof. Guangyu Gao, co-supervised by Prof. Alex K. Qin and Dr. Lin Sun. My work focuses on AI planning for robotics, visual perception, visual localization, and video understanding.
